About

What this means here

Spirituality is a broad concept with room for many perspectives. In general, it includes a sense of connection to something bigger than ourselves, and it typically involves a search for meaning in life. It's a universal human experience — something that touches us all, whether through quiet reflection, nature, faith, or simply a deep sense of aliveness and interconnectedness.

Practice

Ways to practice

  1. 01

    Pray or meditate

    Meditating more often, or joining a meditation group, are simple ways to put this into practice.

  2. 02

    Give

    Growing in this way often comes through helping others — donating, volunteering, or offering your time and skills.

  3. 03

    Live healthier

    This is tied to physical wellbeing too — eating well, moving more, and giving your body real rest.

  4. 04

    Focus inward

    This isn't about appearances — it's about attention turned toward your inner self rather than your outer one.
