Seven yoga asanas

Tadasana (Mountain Pose)

Stand tall with your feet together, arms by your sides, palms facing forward. Lengthen your spine, engage your core, and lift your chest. A grounding pose that improves posture and circulation.

Watch video

Sarvangasana (Shoulder Stand)

Lie on your back and lift your legs and lower body off the ground, supporting your lower back with your hands. Keep your legs straight and toes pointed upward.

Watch video

Halasana (Plow Pose)

From shoulder stand, lower your legs over your head until your feet touch the ground behind you. Keep your hands on your lower back for support.

Watch video

Bhujangasana (Cobra Pose)

Lie on your stomach with palms flat on the ground under your shoulders. Inhale as you lift your chest off the ground, keeping elbows close to your body.

Watch video

Matsyasana (Fish Pose)

Lie on your back with legs extended. Press your forearms and elbows into the ground as you lift your chest toward the ceiling, opening the chest for deeper breathing.

Watch video

Paschimottanasana (Seated Forward Bend)

Sit with legs extended in front of you. Hinge at the hips and reach forward toward your feet or shins. A calming pose that helps quiet the mind.

Watch video

Viparita Karani (Legs-Up-The-Wall Pose)

Sit close to a wall and lie on your back, extending your legs upward along the wall. Relax your arms by your sides and close your eyes for a restorative pause.
